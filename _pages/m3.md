## M3 - Colours 

We noticed the doors of the city were painted in different colours, so we wanted to explore the spatial distributions of these colours. The following colours were decided as approximate categories, reflecting the most prevalent colours we saw: Blue, Red, Green, Brown, Black, Gray, and Other. Each Door was annotated with the closest options, e.g. a turquoise Door being annotated as Green. Doors with no close options were annotated as Other; e.g. a purple Door.
