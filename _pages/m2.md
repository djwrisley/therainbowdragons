## M2 Graffiti & Sticker-Signage Distribution

This map shows the distribution of graffiti and sticker/signage markings on doors across Besançon. Each point on the map represents a photographed door, colour-coded to indicate whether graffiti and/or sticker-signage was present, revealing where these features cluster most densely within the city..

Door images were manually annotated for graffiti, any sticker, or signage. The feature tags (graffiti, sticker-signage) were extracted from our annotated dataset and merged with geolocation data sourced from photo EXIF metadata to create this map.
