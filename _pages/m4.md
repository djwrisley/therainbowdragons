## M4 - Contributors

As this was a group project, there were ten individual contributors. This map shows the doors associated with the contributor who photographed it. When looking at the dataset we identified the individual contributors by the model of their device. Each contributor is represented by a letter from A-H.

We can see how data in academic crowdsourcing, or data in general, always contains bias and subjectivity.
