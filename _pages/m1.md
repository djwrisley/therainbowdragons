## M1 Door Shape Distribution Map

This map visualizes the architectural styles of doors across the center of Besançon, France. We distinguished our dataset of doors between round-top (arched) or non-rounded tops. Each point on the map represents a photographed door, colour coded by the shape category/ blue for round top, and orange for non round top doors, allowing viewers to quickly identify spatial patterns of doors in the city. This visualization can offer a way to think and explore how architectural style correlates with the age and character of different neighborhoods, as well as the visual dialogue between different architectural forms.
