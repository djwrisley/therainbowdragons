## M1 Door Shape Distribution Map

This map visualizes the architectural styles of doors across Besançon. We distinguished our dataset of doors between round-top (arched) or not. Each point on the map represents a photographed door, which is colour coded by the shape category/ blue for round top, and orange for non round top doors,  allowing viewers to quickly identify spatial patterns of doors in the city. This visualization can offer a way to think and explore how architectural style correlates with the age and character of different neighborhoods.
